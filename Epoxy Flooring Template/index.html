How It Works

From Ugly Concrete
to Showroom Floor

Five steps. Zero guesswork. Done in under a week — and built to last longer than anything else you'll invest in for your property.

01

Free Site Assessment

We come to you. We measure the space, test the concrete for moisture and existing coatings, and map out exactly what prep work needs to happen. No guessing, no surprises on install day.

02

Design & Material Selection

You choose from our premium catalog — colors, chips, metallic pigments, topcoat sheen levels. We walk you through every option and guide you to the system that fits your lifestyle and vision.

03

Surface Preparation

Diamond grinding. Crack repair. Moisture mitigation if needed. We prep the concrete to a CSP 3–4 profile — the mechanical bond that makes the difference between a 2-year job and a 20-year job.

04

Professional Installation

Primer coat. Base coat. Decorative broadcast (if applicable). Two coats of commercial-grade topcoat. Every layer timed and applied to specification by our certified installation crew — not day laborers.

05

Final Walkthrough & Warranty

You inspect it with us. We point out every detail, walk you through care instructions, and hand you your 10-year warranty documentation before we ever pick up our tools to leave.
